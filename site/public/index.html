Nous Reel

A free video player that also does the jobs you used to need a website with a five-minute limit for: shrink a huge movie for Plex, stitch CD1 and CD2 together, build subtitles in, check a rip for damage. And it turns any frame into a properly ruined meme.

You're on a phone or tablet. Nous Reel is a program for a Windows or Linux computer. Open this page on your computer to download it: the address is nousreel.lionthroat.com

Using Nous Reel

Open a video with File → Open, or drag it onto the window. Drop an .srt file on it too, and the subtitles come on. The controls fade away while you watch and come back when you move the mouse. It remembers where you stopped.

Meme this frame

Grabs the exact frame on screen at full size. Crop, then Crunch, JPEG rot, Deep fry, Glitch, Crooked… or press Chaos. Impact captions, then Save, Copy, or open it in Nous Halo.

Shrink

Pick a recipe like Plex 720p · small, or open the dials and see what each one does. Estimate the size tries a few seconds first. A 23 GB Blu-ray copy came out at 2 GB.

720p for Plex, from the right-click menu

Right-click a video in File Explorer, choose 720p for Plex, and carry on with your day. Select several to queue them all.

Join parts

Movie came as CD1 and CD2? Open part one and Join parts finds the rest. Matching parts join with no loss in a few minutes.

Tracks & subtitles

Build an .srt into the video so there's nothing to keep track of, drop tracks you don't want, and fix “Unknown” language labels. No quality lost.

Check for damage

Plays the whole file at top speed without showing it and lists every spot where the picture or sound is damaged, with a Jump button. Handy for discs from the thrift store.

F1 in the program lists every shortcut.

Questions

Why does my computer warn me about it?

Microsoft charges a yearly fee for the certificate that makes that warning go away. Nous Reel is free and made by one person, so it skips it. The warning appears once; after that it opens like any other program.

Is it really free? Is there a time limit?

Free, no trial, no subscription, no ads, and no limit on how long a video can be. It's open source: anyone can read exactly what it does on GitHub. The playing is done by mpv and the converting by FFmpeg, both free and open source too.

Does shrinking make it look worse?

A little, in principle; usually not so you'd notice. The Plex 720p recipe is tuned to look very close to the original on a TV. Try Estimate the size and a short test if you're unsure, and the original is never changed or replaced.

Why won't my iTunes or Apple TV purchases open?

Videos bought from Apple are copy-protected (FairPlay), and no program outside Apple's own apps can play or convert them, Plex included. Nous Reel tells you when a file is protected instead of failing with a confusing error. Other files without copy protection work fine.

Can it copy DVDs or Blu-rays?

No. Commercial discs are copy-protected, and Nous Reel stays clear of getting around that. It works with the video files you already have.

How do I get a newer version?

Download and install it the same way. It replaces the old one.

How do I uninstall it?

Windows: Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Nous Reel → Uninstall (the right-click entry goes too). Linux: remove “nous-reel” in Software Manager, or sudo apt remove nous-reel.
